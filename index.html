      Day 01Wankhede, Mumbai2008

      स्थापनाSthapana · the welcome

      Everything starts with No. 1

      Before any puja, people pray to Bappa first. He's Prathama Pujya, which is why his jersey says No. 1. The Paltan had its own first day in 2008: a brand-new team in blue and gold, with Mumbai's own Sachin Tendulkar as captain. There were no trophies yet, just a beginning. And Bappa loves beginnings.

      Every match starts with a toss. Call it.

      Day 02Champions League T20 final2011

      दुर्वाDurva · 21 blades of grass

      The first trophy came from a stand-in

      Bappa's favourite offering isn't gold. It's durva, 21 little blades of plain green grass. In 2011, with Harbhajan Singh leading as stand-in captain, the Paltan went to Chennai and won the Champions League T20. Our very first trophy came when nobody expected it.

      Quick one — who captained that first trophy?

      Tap a name.

      BlessingSmall offerings count. So do stand-ins.

      Day 03Eden Gardens + Delhi2013

      ढोल-ताशाDhol-tasha · the beat

      The year the Paltan found its rhythm

      You hear Ganeshotsav before you see it: dha-dhin-dhin-dha! In 2013, Rohit Sharma became captain partway through the season. He led the Paltan to its first IPL title, beating CSK by 23 runs at Eden Gardens. Months later they also won the Champions League T20 in Delhi, in Sachin's last T20 tournament. That's two cups in one year.

      BlessingWhen you find your rhythm, everyone dances with you.

      Each morning the doorsteps bloom with rangoli, drawn one careful dot at a time. In 2015 the Paltan lost its first four games. Then they rebuilt the season match by match and beat CSK by 41 runs at Eden Gardens for title number two.

      Day 05IPL final · Hyderabad2017

      आरतीAarti · hold the flame

      Everyone held their breath

      During aarti everyone goes quiet and watches one tiny flame, hoping the breeze behaves. That's exactly how the 2017 final felt. It came down to the last over against Rising Pune Supergiant, and the Paltan held its nerve to win by one single run. I didn't breathe for six balls.

      Press and hold. Six balls left. Don't let go.

      BlessingKeep the flame steady when it flickers.

      Day 06IPL final · Hyderabad2019

      मोदकModak · the sweetest bite

      Last ball, two to win

      Ukadiche modak is steamed and stuffed with coconut and jaggery. It's Bappa's favourite. My sweetest bite ever was the 2019 final against CSK. They needed two off the last ball. Lasith Malinga fired in a yorker, Shardul Thakur was out LBW, and the Paltan won by one run again. Fun twist: Shardul joined the Paltan in 2026!

      BlessingThe sweetest things come tightly wrapped.

      Day 07IPL final · Dubai2020

      राजाPandal-hopping · to the Raja

      Lalbaugcha Raja, meet Mumbai cha Raja

      The queue for Lalbaugcha Raja, Mumbai's king since 1934, goes on for hours. The Paltan has its own king too: Rohit Sharma, "Mumbai cha Raja". In 2020 in Dubai, his 68 helped beat Delhi Capitals by 5 wickets. That was title No. 5, back to back, and nobody had ever won five before.

      BlessingA true Raja makes the whole city feel royal.

      Day 08WPL final · Brabourne2023

      गौरीGauri · Shakti comes home

      The first-ever WPL queens

      During Ganeshotsav, Gauri (Bappa's mother) visits Maharashtrian homes, and the celebration gets even bigger. In 2023, Mumbai's women took the field. Harmanpreet Kaur's MI beat Delhi Capitals by 7 wickets at the Brabourne and became the first-ever WPL champions.

      BlessingShakti wins finals.

      Day 09WPL final · Brabourne2025

      जयघोषJayghosh · the loud night

      Tomorrow is goodbye, so tonight is loud

      On the last night every pandal is packed and the chants get louder. In 2025, MI Women won the crown back, beating Delhi Capitals by 8 runs, with Harmanpreet's 66 earning Player of the Match. The men made the playoffs too, and Suryakumar Yadav scored 717 runs, the most by an MI batter in one season. That's trophy number nine.
